🎮 Academic Quest: RPG Semester Predictor
An interactive, dark-mode cyberpunk dashboard that gamifies the engineering university grind. Built around real evaluation rules, this app turns your courses, internals, and attendance tracking into an engaging RPG boss battle.

⚡ Key Features
* **University Prediction Engine:** Tailored for a **50-Mark Internal / 100-Mark Written Exam** system. Automatically calculates the exact score you need on final papers to hit your target GPA.
* **Double-Minimum Guardrails:** Hard-locks a warning if your final exam calculation drops below the mandatory passing score of **40/100**, or if your internals drop below **35/50**.
* **The Backlog Dragon Arena:** Your internal marks serve as your "Attack Power." Maximizing your scores deals direct damage to the boss, draining its HP bar to 0%.
* **Attendance Shield:** Displays an active green shield tracking your eligibility. Dropping below **75% attendance** on any paper breaks your shield and flashes a red **Condonation Risk** warning.
* **Gold Economy & Custom Themes:** Earn **Academic Gold** to unlock custom workspace layouts like *Inferno Mode* or *Retro Matrix*.
* **Local Storage & Reset:** Saves your layout, name, and class profile instantly when refreshed. Includes a "Reset" feature to completely wipe data for a fresh semester start.

🛠️ Tech Stack
* **HTML5** & **Vanilla JavaScript** (Lightweight, fully reactive state engine)
* **Tailwind CSS** (Responsive utility-first grid panels & dark-mode styling)

📦 How to Run
1. Download the `index.html` file.
2. Double-click the file on your computer to run it instantly in any browser—no installation or internet required!
